**Nordic Adventures** is a Wabbajack modlist for Skyrim VR, built to maximize immersion, atmospheric scale, and exploration depth. It pairs a highly optimized, performance-friendly engine base with deep world expansions, so it sidesteps the performance pitfalls of traditional mega-lists and stays smooth. The province gets dramatically bigger without giving up any visual grandeur: sweeping city overhauls, detailed custom landscapes, plenty of new points of interest, and dozens of legendary questlines.
