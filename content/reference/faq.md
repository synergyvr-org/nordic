## The big picture

{{< disclosure title="What is Nordic Adventures VR?" >}}
A feature-rich Skyrim VR mod list built to maximize immersion and exploration depth. It features major city overhauls, custom landscapes, and legendary questlines, utilizing a highly optimized engine base to ensure an incredibly smooth VR experience.
{{< /disclosure >}}

{{< disclosure title="Is this list safe for work (SFW)?" >}}
No, this is an NSFW mod list. It uses {{< nexus 30174 >}}3BA{{< /nexus >}} physics meshes and mature quest expansions ({{< nexus 48912 >}}Amorous Adventures{{< /nexus >}}). You can bypass the adult gameplay loops, but the mature visual assets are baked into the core list.
{{< /disclosure >}}

{{< disclosure title="What major quests and followers are included?" >}}
- **The core pillar**: {{< nexus 11802 >}}Legacy of the Dragonborn{{< /nexus >}} is fully integrated, for the ultimate museum relic hunt.
- **DLC-sized quests**: {{< nexus 4155 >}}Clockwork{{< /nexus >}}, {{< nexus 1179 >}}The Forgotten City{{< /nexus >}}, {{< nexus 45565 >}}Wyrmstooth{{< /nexus >}}, {{< nexus 133579 >}}Olenveld{{< /nexus >}}, {{< nexus 131 >}}Skyrim Underground{{< /nexus >}}, and more.
- **Companions**: Fully voiced followers including {{< nexus 1461 >}}INIGO{{< /nexus >}}, {{< nexus 85298 >}}Gore{{< /nexus >}}, {{< nexus 11278 >}}Auri (Song of the Green){{< /nexus >}}, {{< nexus 4718 >}}Recorder{{< /nexus >}}, {{< nexus 108546 >}}Malryn{{< /nexus >}}, and {{< nexus 141554 >}}The Iceheart Sisters{{< /nexus >}}, plus the enormous cast of {{< nexus 29194 >}}Interesting NPCs{{< /nexus >}}.

## Visuals and customization

{{< disclosure title="What body and armor systems does it use?" >}}
It uses 3BA and {{< nexus 46311 >}}HIMBO{{< /nexus >}} bodies. High-fidelity armors ({{< nexus 20199 >}}Cathedral Armory{{< /nexus >}}, NordwarUA, Frankly HD, DX Fashions) are dynamically distributed via {{< nexus 59121 >}}SPID{{< /nexus >}} to ensure seamless integration. NPC overhauls are custom-patched for VR to completely eliminate neck seams.
{{< /disclosure >}}

{{< disclosure title="Can I customize or add mods to the list?" >}}
Yes! Unlike more rigid mega-lists, Nordic's custom patches are split into modules, so an experienced modder can usually tailor the list by removing one specific patch rather than breaking the entire load order. (Note: customizing voids official troubleshooting support.)
{{< /disclosure >}}
